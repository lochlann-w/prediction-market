## Global shipping

### Resource: [Polymarket - Global shipping](https://polymarket.com/search?_q=global-shipping)
*Accessed 31/9/26*

### Supporting resource: [Kalshi - Global shipping](https://kalshi.com/search?q=global%20shipping&order_by=querymatch)
*Accessed 31/9/26*

#### Findings

- Highly political market
- Low amount of contracts
- Some contracts with exceptionally high volume, 20M+ USD
- Majority of contracts with low volume.
- High liquidity
- Largely based on Strait of Hormuz & Suez Canal
- Independant data source

### Conclusion

High volume contracts are non repeating. Resembles election result prediction contracts. Research for Global shipping will not continue past this pass, as available contracts vastly differ over time.
